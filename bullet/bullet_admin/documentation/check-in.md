# Check-in

Check-in records that a team has arrived at the venue. It is optional and only helps organisers keep track of arrivals. Teams do not need to be checked in manually to compete: the system automatically marks them as checked in when they submit their first problem.

Check-in is separate from email confirmation and admission from the waiting list.

Operators can check in teams at their assigned venues. Venue, country and branch administrators can also check in teams within their scope.

## Prepare the desk

Before teams arrive, make sure the check-in staff can access the correct competition and venue. Assign team numbers and prepare the [tearoffs](/admin/documentation/tearoffs/) before handing out sets.

For a paper reference, open the venue's "Team list PDF" tab. This list includes competing teams with assigned numbers. You can also use the [Teams page](/admin/documentation/teams/) to filter by venue and status.

## Check in a team manually

1. Find the team on the "Teams" page and open its edit page.
2. Confirm its school and team identifier, especially if the school has several teams.
3. Check the contestant names and problem language. Correct any changes the team reports.
4. Check photo consent with the team and record it according to your local rules.
5. Select "Checked in?" and save.
6. Give the team the tearoff set matching its assigned number.

Return to the team list to confirm the status is "Checked in".

Once checked in, a team can no longer change its own details through its private email link. Organisers can still make corrections.

## Problems at the desk

### The team cannot be found

Check the selected competition and venue, then search by school or contact details. Searching contestant names is not supported. Operators cannot see contact details, so an administrator may need to help.

The number prefixed with `#` is the database ID, not necessarily the number printed on the tearoffs. Check the assigned team number before handing out paper sets.

### The team is unconfirmed or on the waiting list

Ask a venue administrator to resolve its registration status before handing out a competition set. Checking a team in is not a substitute for confirming its registration or moving it off the waiting list.

See [registration](/admin/documentation/registration/) and [waiting lists](/admin/documentation/waiting-list/) for those steps.

### The team needs another number or venue

Operators cannot change the school, venue or assigned number. Ask an administrator to make the change.

Numbers must be unique within a venue. Moving a team keeps its number, so an administrator may need to resolve a conflict at the destination venue. Any paper set must match the team's saved venue and number.

If you use a backup tearoff set, assign its number to the team before the set is used. Do not simply give the team another team's papers.

### A team was checked in by mistake

Open its edit page, clear "Checked in?" and save. Check the status on the team list afterwards.

Do not check in absent teams just to complete the list. The flag should reflect who is actually at the venue.
